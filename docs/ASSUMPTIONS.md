# Assumptions

The brief leaves some things open. Each item below records the choice we made, so it can be checked and changed. The TDD cites them by number.

| #   | Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A1  | The daily overdue CSV has these columns: `loan_id, borrower_name, business_type, phone, address, pincode, outstanding, dpd, emi_amount, last_payment_date, last_payment_amount, branch_code`.                                                                                                                                              | The brief's sample shows only 6 fields. Pincode-based assignment and the call and map buttons need the rest.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| A2  | There is one import per day. Re-uploading the same as-of date replaces that day's snapshot (the earlier batch is marked superseded). Days must be imported in order: a day older than the latest committed import is refused. A branch manager's upload applies only to their branch's rows; the head of collections imports all branches. | Correcting a bad export shouldn't create duplicate history, and importing out of order would rewind assignments and promise outcomes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A3  | An officer's territory is a set of pincodes. A loan stays with its current officer while that officer covers its pincode; otherwise it goes to the covering officer with the fewest loans. The 25-loan cap applies to the **daily list** (top 25 by priority), not to ownership.                                                           | The brief asks for assignment by pincode and workload. Capping ownership would leave overdue loans without an owner on heavy days, and continuity keeps one face per borrower.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| A4  | Contact is allowed from 08:00 to 18:59:59 IST, every day of the week.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | RBI recovery rules limit contact to 8 am–7 pm. All borrowers are in Madhya Pradesh, so IST applies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| A5  | There are at most 3 contact attempts per loan per day. The limit is configurable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | RBI rules forbid harassment. A cap makes that enforceable rather than a matter of policy alone. It counts per loan. Each loan on the export gets its own borrower record, because the file carries no borrower ID to match on. So a person with two loans could be contacted up to six times a day. A true per-person limit needs a borrower ID from the loan system. |
| A6  | A loan "rolls" when it is 1–30 DPD on day D and 31+ DPD on day D+30.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | This matches the brief's 1–30 → 31–60 roll-rate metric. This matches the brief's 1–30 to 31–60: days overdue rise at most one a day, so a loan at 30 or under on day D is at 60 or under on day D+30. The app checks for 31+ rather than capping at 60, because a loan above 60 could only come from a wrong export, and a cap would hide that.                       |
| A7  | A promise is **kept** if payments of at least 90% of the promised amount arrive by the promise date + 1 day. Less than that, but more than zero, is **partial**. Nothing is **broken**.                                                                                                                                                    | The loan system export runs a day behind. Small shortfalls (for example, rounding) shouldn't count as broken promises.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| A8  | A payment is detected when `last_payment_date` moves forward (amount from `last_payment_amount`), or else when `outstanding` drops, dated the day before (export lag). A loan that **drops off** the overdue list is treated as cured: its arrears were cleared, so a pending promise on it counts as kept.                                | The export is the only source of payment truth, and it lists only loans with arrears; receipts are out of scope. A row with an error is not treated as missing: its loan and any promise on it stay as they were until a corrected file arrives.                                                                                                                      |
| A9  | The priority model is trained on synthetic history generated by the seeder. It must be retrained on the client's real repayment history before go-live.                                                                                                                                                                                    | No real data is available or allowed in the repo.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| A10 | Of the loans that roll, 20% turn NPA, with a 35% loss given default. Both figures come from the brief, and they drive the rupee-at-risk figures.                                                                                                                                                                                           | These are the client's own numbers: 0.20 × 0.35 × outstanding is the cost of one roll.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| A11 | There are two branches: Indore Central (pincodes 4520xx) and Pithampur (454775 and nearby), with 6–8 officers including Rahul Chouhan, Neha Solanki and Imran Qureshi.                                                                                                                                                                     | The client is based in Indore. The officer names come from the brief's sample data. The demo uses fewer officers than the brief's 55 on purpose, to stay easy to review. A test runs assignment at full scale: 55 officers and about 500 loans.                                                                                                                       |
| A12 | A **Dispute** outcome pauses contact for that loan until a branch manager clears it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Chasing a disputed amount can turn into harassment. A person should review it first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| A13 | Officers use Android phones running Chrome. Sending happens while the app is open: at each log, when signal returns, when the app comes to the foreground, and on a timer.                                                                                                                                                                 | The brief describes low-end phones, and Android dominates that segment in India. Officers open the app all day, so background sending when it's closed adds little (see the cut list).                                                                                                                                                                                |
| A14 | Borrowers are placeholders ("Company B (packaging unit)"), and phone numbers come from a fake 90000-xxxxx range.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The repository must not contain real personal data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| A15 | Seeded dates are relative to the day the seed runs. For example, LN-10293's promise falls in 3 days, and LN-11930's promise broke 2 days ago.                                                                                                                                                                                              | The demo then looks the same whenever it is reviewed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A16 | The officer UI defaults to Hindi, with English available as a toggle. The manager UI defaults to English.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | The brief requires a Hindi interface. Head-office reporting is typically in English.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| A17 | Money is stored as whole rupees.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The loan system export has no paise, and whole rupees avoid floating-point rounding.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| A18 | List order: loans at 1–30 days overdue come first, then 31+. Within each, most rupees at stake first. At 1–30 that is P(roll) × outstanding × 0.07; at 31+ it is P(cure) × outstanding × 0.07.                                                                                                                                             | The client is buying a lower roll rate. Past 30 days the roll has already happened, so what's left to influence is the chance of cure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| A19 | A loan with an open promise isn't chased until the promised date; it waits in its own section.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Chasing someone before the date they gave you is the kind of pressure RBI's rules are aimed at.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| A20 | "Paid" is recorded as reported by the officer and doesn't change any balance. The next import confirms it. A promise defaults to one EMI.                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Cash receipts are out of scope, and the loan system stays the source of truth.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| A21 | A manager's move pins a loan's position for that day only, with a reason the officer sees. A manual assignment stays with that officer through later imports, even outside their pincodes.                                                                                                                                                 | The score is advisory, so a person can always overrule it. The morning's fresh ranking should normally stand, while a deliberate reassignment shouldn't be undone by the next upload.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A22 | Borrowers are reminded at 10 am the day before and 9 am on the promised day; the officer at 8:30 am that day. Reminders stop once a promise settles or the loan is in dispute.                                                                                                                                                             | Inside the RBI window, early enough to act on, and never repeated after the point has passed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| A23 | "About to roll" means 25–30 days overdue on the latest list. A visit is flagged when it has no GPS fix or is over 500 m from the borrower's address.                                                                                                                                                                                       | Close enough to act on and short enough to read. 500 m allows for a weak GPS fix and an approximate address; it's a prompt to look, not an accusation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| A24 | Import previews that are never committed are deleted after 24 hours.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | They hold borrowers' names and phone numbers, and an abandoned upload has no reason to keep them (DPDP).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| A25 | The two extra reminder templates (a missed promise, an overdue instalment with no promise) are our wording, and the lender's compliance team would approve them before use.                                                                                                                                                                |
| A26 | "Nothing sends without the officer's tap" applies to AI drafts. The automatic promise reminders the brief also asks for go out on schedule, because they use fixed approved wording.                                                                                                                                                       |
| A27 | A reminder message counts as a contact: it needs the 8 am–7 pm window and counts towards the limit of 3 contacts a day.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A28 | If at least 5 loans and over 20% of the previous list are missing from a file, it may be incomplete, so a manager must confirm they are no longer overdue before it can commit. 20% comes from the simulated history, where normal days peak at 13.5% of a branch's list. It should be retuned on real data.                               |
| A29 | If today's import is late, officers keep working the latest list, marked with its date, rather than an empty one. Managers are prompted to upload it. A day's calls on yesterday's list beat a blank screen, as long as the officer knows the figures are older.                                                                           |
